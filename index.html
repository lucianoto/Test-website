Quick start

If you have just been given this device and want the short version, this is it. Every step is covered in more detail in the sections above.

  1. Wash your hands

    Soap and water for at least 20 seconds before you touch any part of the device. This is the single most effective step for preventing lung infection.

  2. Check the parts are clean and dry

    Any moisture left inside a device is somewhere bacteria can grow. If a part is damp, cracked, cloudy, or discoloured, do not use it.

  3. Assemble and run your treatment

    Follow the sequence your care team gave you — the order of inhaled medicines and airway clearance matters. See the device guide.

  4. Take the device apart and wash it right away

    Rinse and wash after every use, then disinfect on the schedule your manufacturer specifies. Follow the cleaning instructions supplied with your device.

  5. Air-dry completely, then store covered

    Never towel-dry with cloth, and never store parts while still damp.

Never share equipment

Devices, mouthpieces, masks, and tubing must never be shared between people — including between family members who both have CF. Sharing is a direct route for transmitting Pseudomonas aeruginosa, Burkholderia cepacia complex, and other organisms that are difficult to eradicate once established.

When to contact your care team

This site does not replace your CF centre. Contact them promptly if you notice:

  • More coughing than usual, or a change in the colour, thickness, or amount of sputum
  • New or worsening breathlessness, chest tightness, or wheeze
  • Fever, unusual tiredness, loss of appetite, or unexplained weight loss
  • Coughing up blood
  • A drop in your home lung-function readings, if you track them
  • Visible mould, film, or a persistent smell in device parts that cleaning does not remove
Emergency

Severe breathing difficulty, coughing up a large volume of blood, or chest pain with breathlessness needs emergency care immediately — do not wait for a clinic appointment.

Medical disclaimer. This guide is educational and does not replace advice from your cystic fibrosis care team, or the instructions supplied by the device manufacturer. Where this site and your manufacturer's instructions differ, follow the manufacturer. Always confirm changes to your treatment or cleaning routine with your clinician.
