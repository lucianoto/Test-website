How the Device Works

This section is being written. It will cover how the patch performs a sweat test, how it delivers medication through the skin, and what each layer of the device does.

Development status

The CF Device is a research prototype under active development. It has not been approved or cleared by any regulatory authority, is not available to patients, and must not be used to diagnose or treat cystic fibrosis. Nothing on this page describes a product you can obtain or use.

Cystic fibrosis is diagnosed by sweat chloride testing at a specialist centre, supported by genetic testing. The Resources section lists centres that provide it.
