A note on format

Every page on this site already has a print stylesheet: navigation and interactive chrome are stripped out, cards and tables avoid breaking across pages, and external links print with their full URL. You can produce a serviceable PDF of any section today with your browser's Print → Save as PDF.

The dedicated printables above would go further — single-page layouts with writing space, designed for the page rather than the screen.

Nothing here is tracked

This is a static site with no analytics, no cookies, and no server. Downloading a file records nothing about you anywhere.

Medical disclaimer. This guide is educational and does not replace advice from your cystic fibrosis care team, or the instructions supplied by the device manufacturer.
